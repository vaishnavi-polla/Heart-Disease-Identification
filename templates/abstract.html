ABSTRACT

Heart disease is one of the major health problems affecting people worldwide. Early identification of heart disease can help in taking timely medical care and reducing health risks.


Our project, Heart Disease Identification, is a web-based system developed to identify the possibility of heart disease using patient health information. The system analyzes the entered health parameters using a machine learning classification model and provides a prediction result.We evaluated Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) and compared their performance using accuracy and other evaluation measures. Among the tested algorithms, Logistic Regression achieved the highest accuracy of 91.67%, making it the final selected model for our Heart Disease Identification system.


The system also stores patient information and prediction records in a database for authorized access. The main objective of this project is to provide a simple, fast, and user-friendly system that can support the early identification of possible heart disease.
