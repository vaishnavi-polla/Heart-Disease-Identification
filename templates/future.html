FUTURE SCOPE

The Heart Disease Identification system has several possibilities for future improvement. The system can be trained with larger and more diverse datasets to improve prediction reliability. Additional machine learning models can also be evaluated to identify the most suitable model for different patient data. More health parameters and patient medical history can be included to provide better risk analysis. The application can further be developed into a mobile-friendly platform and can be integrated with healthcare systems for authorized access to patient records. Enhanced security and privacy features can also be implemented to protect patient information. In the future, the system can act as a supportive tool for early identification of possible heart disease and assist healthcare professionals, while the final medical decision remains with qualified healthcare professionals.
