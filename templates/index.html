UNDERSTANDING YOUR HEART

About Heart Disease

❤️

What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease refers to conditions that affect the heart and the way it functions. It can affect the blood vessels, heart rhythm, heart muscles, or other parts of the cardiovascular system.

⚠️

Common Risk Factors

Factors such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking, physical inactivity, unhealthy eating habits, diabetes, age, and family history may increase the risk of heart disease.

🫀

Warning Signs

Some warning signs may include chest discomfort, shortness of breath, unusual tiredness, dizziness, or discomfort in other areas of the upper body. Symptoms can vary from person to person.

💙

Take Care of Your Heart

Regular physical activity, balanced nutrition, adequate rest, avoiding tobacco, managing stress, and regular health check-ups can support overall heart health.
